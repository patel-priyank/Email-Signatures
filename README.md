# Email Signatures

"Best regards" has never once been the best. Press a key and get a randomly picked sign-off: somewhere between casually cool and completely unhinged, with pop-culture nods, nerdy farewells and the occasional bit of dry sarcasm along the way. Copy it into your next email and let the recipient wonder if you're okay. Ranges from "Stay frosty" to "Exit, pursued by a bear." No corporate pleasantries were harmed in the making of this app.

If it made you smirk, share it with your colleagues. Their inboxes deserve better too.

## Add your own

Got a sign-off that deserves to be out in the wild? Add it to [`signatures.txt`](signatures.txt): one per line, in alphabetical order, with no trailing comma (the app adds one). Keep it fun and keep it short, ideally something that fits on one line on a phone (about 25 characters). The easiest way is on GitHub itself: open the file, click the pencil icon, make your edit and choose "Propose changes". GitHub forks the repo and opens a pull request for you, and I'll take a look and merge it.
